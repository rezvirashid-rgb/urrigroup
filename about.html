About the Group

Built to move Bangladesh forward.

One promise became a family of businesses — each one carrying the same standards of trust, professionalism and forward motion.

Our Story

It started with a simple promise.

Urri Group started with one promise — get people where they need to go — and is growing into a family of Bangladeshi businesses built on the same idea: move people, goods, ideas and opportunities forward, reliably.

Mission

To build trusted, professionally run businesses across sectors that shape everyday life — travel, trade, technology, property, finance and care.

Leadership

Built by an experienced leadership team.

Urri Group brings together professionals with broad experience across travel, technology, financial services, marketing, international business, trade, operations and commercial management.
